## Unlimited Electricity

[![](https://steamuserimages-a.akamaihd.net/ugc/2450613629164756082/2DFDDEA01CBA2873358486805C093F3357D2AD1F/)](https://steamuserimages-a.akamaihd.net/ugc/2450613629164756082/2DFDDEA01CBA2873358486805C093F3357D2AD1F/)In order to have Unlimited electricity we will need to use the Map Editor, for us to use that we will need to use Console again.  
Open the console and enter /editor after we enter that Command a small window will appear

[![](https://steamuserimages-a.akamaihd.net/ugc/2450613629164762789/C8464283827671006D588915FA9C63A20399F391/)](https://steamuserimages-a.akamaihd.net/ugc/2450613629164762789/C8464283827671006D588915FA9C63A20399F391/)

- Go to the Entities Tab
- Go to the Unsorted tab
- Find the “Electric Energy Interface”
- Place the “Electric Energy Interface” anywhere on the map
- Close the Map Editor by entering the /editor command again on the Console

Now we have Unlimited Electricity on our new sandbox map. You can edit the “Electric Energy Interface” Settings by clicking on it.

## Lab Tiles and Global Electric Network

In the last part of this guide we are going to turn the map Surface into Lab Tiles and enable the Global Electric Network

[![](https://steamuserimages-a.akamaihd.net/ugc/2450613629164768010/47711EBC1B0CF9AB34C4CE9C5C5A77E92018623D/)](https://steamuserimages-a.akamaihd.net/ugc/2450613629164768010/47711EBC1B0CF9AB34C4CE9C5C5A77E92018623D/)

To turn the surface of our map into Lab Tiles we need to- Open Console again and enter /editor command
- Go to “Surfaces” Tab
- Click in “Removes all entities”
- Click in “Fill with lab tiles”
- Check “Generate new chunks with lab tiles” **THIS IS VERY IMPORTANT** otherwise new chunks will not generate with lab tiles
- Check “Global electric network” to allow the use of your electricity without using electric poles
